English

1. Introduction

ZenFlow ("we", "our", "the app") is a privacy-first journaling application. ZenFlow has no user accounts, no profiles and no servers of its own. This page describes exactly what the app stores, what it never stores, and the few requests that leave your device.

2. Data We Collect

ZenFlow collects no data. We operate no servers and receive nothing from the app — there is nothing to sign in to, and no copy of your journal exists anywhere except on your device. This matches the Data Not Collected declaration on our App Store Connect App Privacy form and the empty collected-data list in the app's privacy manifest.

¹ Your coordinates are never sent to us. They are rounded on your device and passed to your operating system's own geocoding service to obtain a place name — see section 3.

3. What Leaves Your Device

Only three things ever leave your device, and none of them reach us:

RequestGoes ToContains
Place-name lookupYour OS geocoding service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android) A coordinate rounded to two decimal places (~1 km), with no identifier attached
Purchases & restoresApple App Store / Google Play Handled entirely by the store under its own privacy policy
Legal page linksThis website, in your browser An ordinary page request you initiate by tapping a link

The place-name lookup happens only if you grant location access, and only when an entry captures context. ZenFlow rounds the coordinate before using it, keeps the result on your device, and never attaches a name, device ID or any other identifier to the request. Deny location access and the request never happens at all.

Beyond these, ZenFlow makes no network requests of its own. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no cloud backup, and no content or font downloaded at runtime.

4. Health Data

With your explicit permission, ZenFlow reads heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, blood oxygen, respiratory rate and body temperature from Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) to colour your Aura sphere and add context to entries.

Access is read-only: the app never writes anything back to Health, and it does not request write permission. Readings are stored only in the local database on your device. They are never transmitted to us, never shared with advertisers or analytics platforms, and are used for nothing but your own journal context. Declining health access leaves the rest of the app fully usable.

5. Voice Notes & Transcription

Voice notes are recorded into the app's own storage on your device. Transcription runs on the device; ZenFlow explicitly requires on-device recognition, so your audio is not sent to Apple's or Google's speech servers. If a device does not have the on-device language pack installed, voice input is disabled and you are told why — the app does not quietly fall back to server-based recognition. Deleting an entry deletes its recording.

Every permission is optional. Journaling works fully without any of them.

7. In-App Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. ZenFlow never receives or stores your payment information, and no account is created. What you have unlocked is recorded only on your device.

8. No Analytics, Advertising or Crash Reporting

ZenFlow contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry or similar). It does not track your behaviour, build a usage profile, or assign you an identifier. The app's privacy manifest declares no tracking and no tracking domains.

Typefaces are bundled inside the app, so ZenFlow does not fetch fonts from a content delivery network on launch.

Earlier versions offered an optional Google Drive backup and optional crash reporting. Both features, and the third-party SDKs behind them, have been removed from the app entirely. ZenFlow no longer has any cloud backup, and no longer asks you to sign in with a Google account.

9. Children's Privacy

ZenFlow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Data Deletion

ZenFlow creates no account, so there is nothing for us to delete and nothing to request from us. You can permanently remove your data by deleting entries within the app — which also deletes their recordings — or by uninstalling the app, which removes its database and files from your device.

11. Changes & Contact

We will notify users via an in-app notice when this policy changes materially. For privacy questions: kaan.yikik@gmail.com

4. Sağlık Verileri

Açık izninizle ZenFlow, Aura küresini renklendirmek ve kayıtlarınıza bağlam eklemek için Apple HealthKit (iOS) veya Health Connect (Android) üzerinden kalp atış hızı, kalp hızı değişkenliği, uyku, kandaki oksijen, solunum hızı ve vücut sıcaklığını okur.

Erişim yalnızca okumadır: uygulama Sağlık'a hiçbir şey geri yazmaz ve yazma izni istemez. Ölçümler yalnızca cihazınızdaki yerel veritabanında saklanır. Bize iletilmez, reklamcılarla veya analitik platformlarla paylaşılmaz ve kendi günlük bağlamınız dışında hiçbir şey için kullanılmaz. Sağlık erişimini reddetseniz de uygulamanın geri kalanı eksiksiz çalışır.

5. Sesli Notlar & Metne Dökme

Sesli notlar cihazınızdaki uygulama deposuna kaydedilir. Metne dökme işlemi cihaz üzerinde çalışır; ZenFlow cihaz içi tanımayı açıkça zorunlu kılar, bu yüzden sesiniz Apple'ın veya Google'ın konuşma sunucularına gönderilmez. Cihazda o dilin cihaz içi paketi yüklü değilse sesli giriş devre dışı bırakılır ve nedeni size söylenir — uygulama sessizce sunucu tabanlı tanımaya düşmez. Bir kaydı sildiğinizde ses dosyası da silinir.

Önceki sürümlerde isteğe bağlı Google Drive yedekleme ve isteğe bağlı çökme raporlaması vardı. Her iki özellik de, arkalarındaki üçüncü taraf SDK'larla birlikte uygulamadan tamamen kaldırıldı. ZenFlow'da artık bulut yedekleme yoktur ve sizden Google hesabınızla giriş yapmanız istenmez.
