ZenFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated / Son güncelleme: 2026-06-20
English Türkçe

English

1. Introduction

ZenFlow ("we", "our", "the app") is a privacy-first journaling application. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how the app works.

2. Data We Collect

ZenFlow collects no data on our servers. All information you enter stays exclusively on your device.

Data TypeWhere StoredShared
Journal text entriesLocal device (Isar DB)Never
Audio recordingsLocal device filesystemNever
Heart rate & health metricsLocal device (Isar DB)Never
Location (city-level)Local device (Isar DB)Never
Weather dataFetched from OpenWeatherMap API¹Never
Backup filesUser's own Google Drive²Never

¹ A city-level GPS coordinate is sent to OpenWeatherMap solely to fetch current weather. No personal identifier accompanies this request.
² Cloud backup is optional and fully user-controlled. Files are AES-256 encrypted on your device before upload. We never access your Google Drive.

3. Health Data

ZenFlow reads heart rate, sleep, and related metrics from Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android) only when you open the app and with your explicit permission. This data is never transmitted to our servers, never shared with advertisers or analytics platforms, and is used solely to enrich your journal entry context.

4. Permissions

PermissionPurpose
MicrophoneVoice note recording (stored locally)
Location (approximate)City-level weather context for entries
Health / HealthKitHeart rate, sleep, HRV read access
NotificationsWeekly wellness summary; smart BPM alerts
InternetWeather API calls and optional backup only
Google Sign-InOptional encrypted backup to your Google Drive

5. In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by the Google Play Store (or Apple App Store). ZenFlow does not receive or store your payment information.

6. Analytics & Crash Reporting

ZenFlow contains no advertising or analytics SDK (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) and does not track your behavior or build any usage profile.

Optional crash reporting via Sentry may be enabled in release builds to capture anonymous crash diagnostics. When enabled, it sends no personal data and no usage traces — only technical crash stack traces needed to fix bugs.

7. Children's Privacy

ZenFlow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Data Deletion

ZenFlow creates no account. You can permanently remove all data by deleting entries within the app or uninstalling the app from your device.

9. Changes & Contact

We will notify users via an in-app notice when this policy changes materially. For privacy questions: kaan.yikik@gmail.com

5. Uygulama İçi Satın Alımlar

Satın alımlar Google Play Store (veya Apple App Store) tarafından işlenir. ZenFlow ödeme bilgilerinizi almaz veya saklamaz.

6. Analitik & Çökme Raporlaması

ZenFlow reklam veya analitik SDK'sı içermez (Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel vb. yok) ve davranışınızı izlemez, kullanım profili oluşturmaz.

Sürüm yapılarında, anonim çökme tanılaması için isteğe bağlı Sentry çökme raporlaması etkinleştirilebilir. Etkinleştirildiğinde hiçbir kişisel veri ve hiçbir kullanım izi göndermez — yalnızca hataları düzeltmek için gereken teknik çökme yığın izlerini iletir.

7. Çocukların Gizliliği

ZenFlow 13 yaşın altındaki çocuklara yönelik değildir. Çocuklardan bilerek bilgi toplamayız.

8. Veri Silme

ZenFlow hesap oluşturmaz. Uygulama içinde kayıtları silerek veya uygulamayı cihazınızdan kaldırarak tüm verilerinizi kalıcı olarak kaldırabilirsiniz.
